Research projects

Models, systems, and benchmarks for spoken communication.

01 Streaming speech translation

End-to-end model

S3-S2ST

A streaming Thinker–Talker model that learns both what to translate and when to speak from semantically aligned supervision. A playback-buffer policy adjusts the balance between translation quality and response delay.

PaperCode ↗Model ↗

Real-time system

X-Translator

A multilingual simultaneous interpretation system that connects streaming ASR, machine translation, and speaker-conditioned TTS. It preserves speaker identity in the output, with reported end-to-end latency of 3–5 seconds.

PaperCode ↗Live demo ↗

Evaluation benchmark

OpenSTBench

A shared evaluation protocol for offline and streaming speech-to-text and speech-to-speech translation. It measures translation accuracy alongside speech quality, speaker and emotion preservation, timing consistency, and latency.

PaperCode & data ↗

Related work: training compact machine translation models with LLM-generated target text, and studying how coverage, data quality, and sequence-level supervision affect the results.

02 Privacy & traceability

Model attribution

Traceable TTS

Jointly trains a speech generator and discriminator to attribute synthetic speech to its source model without embedding an explicit watermark, while preserving speech quality.

PaperProject repo ↗

Speaker & content privacy

Utility-Preserved Speech Anonymization

Combines flow-matching speaker anonymization with generative content editing to reduce identity and content leakage while retaining the data’s usefulness for training ASR and TTS systems.

03 Real-time speech interaction

Streaming voice conversion

X-VC

Adapts speech to an unseen target voice while preserving the words. It performs one-step conversion in a neural codec’s latent space and processes audio in streaming chunks.

PaperCode ↗Audio samples ↗

Full-duplex dialogue

SoulX-Duplug

A plug-in module that uses streaming ASR to predict dialogue states and manage when a voice assistant should listen or speak. An accompanying benchmark extends bilingual coverage for turn-taking evaluation.
