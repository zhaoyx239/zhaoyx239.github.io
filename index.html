Yuxiang Zhao 赵宇翔

I work on speech translation and speech security, with a focus on how systems translate speech in real time, how we evaluate them beyond text accuracy, and how we protect or trace the speech they generate.

Selected research projects

Projects I contributed to most directly. The full publication list is below.

01 Streaming speech translation

S3-S2ST

  • Co-developed an end-to-end Thinker–Talker model that translates incoming speech incrementally and generates spoken output as it listens.
  • Used semantic alignment to train the model to learn what to translate and when to speak, and added a playback-buffer policy to balance translation quality against delay.
Code Model

X-Translator

  • Co-built a multilingual interpretation pipeline that connects streaming speech recognition, machine translation, and speaker-conditioned speech synthesis.
  • Helped release a live demo that preserves the source speaker’s voice across languages, with reported end-to-end latency of 3–5 seconds.
Code Live demo

OpenSTBench

  • Co-developed a shared evaluation protocol for speech-to-text and speech-to-speech translation in both offline and streaming settings.
  • Helped extend evaluation beyond translation accuracy to cover speech quality, speaker and emotion preservation, timing consistency, and latency.
Code & data

02 Privacy & traceability

Traceable TTS

  • Proposed a watermark-free approach that jointly trains a speech generator and discriminator to attribute synthetic speech to its source model.
  • Evaluated whether attribution generalizes across domains without sacrificing speech quality.
Project repo

Utility-Preserved Speech Anonymization

  • Co-built a pipeline that combines flow-matching speaker anonymization with generative editing of sensitive spoken content.
  • Evaluated whether the anonymized speech still works as training data for speech recognition and synthesis while reducing identity and content leakage.
